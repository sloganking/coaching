Privacy Policy

Last updated August 20, 2026.

What this covers

This policy describes how Logan King and SLKing, LLC ("we," "us") handle information you share when you contact us, book a call, or communicate with us about coaching services, including by text message.

What we collect

We collect only what you give us directly:

  • Contact details you provide (name, email address, phone number) when you reach out, book a call, or start a conversation.
  • The content of messages you send us by email, text, or through a contact form.
  • Basic scheduling information if you book a session (date, time, chosen service).

This site does not use cookies, analytics scripts, or any third-party tracking. We are not collecting anything about you just from visiting this page.

How we use it

Information you provide is used only to respond to you, schedule and deliver coaching services, and communicate with you about those services. We do not use it for anything else.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you text us or opt in to receive texts from us, that phone number and the content of those messages are used solely to communicate with you about coaching services.

No mobile information, including text messaging originator opt-in data and consent, is shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories of information sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent — that information is never shared with third parties, under any circumstance.

You can stop receiving texts from us at any time by replying STOP to any message. Message and data rates may apply.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. We may use standard service providers (such as our phone/SMS provider, calendar tools, and email) strictly to deliver the communication and scheduling you've requested — none of them are permitted to use your information for their own marketing purposes.

Data retention and security

We keep contact and communication records only as long as needed to provide services and maintain reasonable business records, and we take reasonable steps to keep that information secure. If you'd like your information deleted, contact us and we'll take care of it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Reply STOP to opt out of texts, or just tell us directly — we'll honor it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
