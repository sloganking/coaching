It's difficult to summarize the enormous benefit that working with Logan has had on my life. While a big part of our work together was centered around sexual embodiment and working through old patterns of shame surrounding that, the benefits of this work has spilled over into almost all aspects of my life.

I sought out Logan around a time that I was grappling with deep shame, isolation and a feeling of impending doom, and that the things I was facing then would define me and the rest of my life, solidifying into my eventual fate. It is really difficult to describe the feelings around this, but I felt a constant sense of being trapped at all angles without a possibility of change. A feeling of being completely frozen and helpless. Basically, I felt like I would never share an intimate moment with a woman again. Little did I know that just a few months later, I would.

I've learned that our thoughts and fears truly are deceiving and especially convincing in how real they feel when we are in them. This pattern of feeling frozen persisted for years until the day I talked to Logan. He immediately made me feel less like a unique case and more that my feelings were a completely normal aspect of my past conditionings. But most importantly that I wasn't my conditioning.

Logan has a special skill of seeing beyond the outer layer of your fear based, triggered state, and into what your heart and being really desire deep down, a part of yourself that still believes that your desires are possible. After a few months of working with him, I felt equipped with tools and strategies to be able to hold those old feelings with care and tenderness while going after the juicy and healing connections that I truly wanted. I learned inner resilience and fortitude. In just 6 months with working with Logan, I've transformed from a state of helpless near bed-ridden fear and anxiety, to standing proud in who I am in both strengths and vulnerabilities. While I am still growing and learning I feel extremely proud of myself that I've learned how to broadcast my desires in the company of women who I desire to have connections with, rather than contracting and shutting down like I did before.

Through this work, I've been able to strip away old beliefs that I previously held as fact. I now see clearer than ever that being attractive is available to everyone and that it's not about attaining perfect looks and outer status but is much more about broadcasting confidently and clearly who we are to those around us and letting them decide whether we have something to offer them. I've been pleasantly surprised to learn that quite often I do have quite a bit to offer. I am so grateful to Logan for his service and seeing potential that I previously couldn't see in myself.

I hope that if any part of my story resonated with you, that you will also consider working with Logan to become that much closer to living a life you never thought was possible.
